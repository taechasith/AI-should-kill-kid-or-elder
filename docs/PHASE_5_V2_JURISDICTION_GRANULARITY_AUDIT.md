# Phase 5 v2 jurisdiction granularity audit

All 25 jurisdictions remain in the v1 top-level selection frame. No operative
scope—national, subnational, or mixed—has yet been assigned in v2 because that
requires a jurisdiction-specific official-source review. The machine-readable
audit is `data/legal/phase5_v2/jurisdiction_granularity_audit_v2.json`.

In particular, no state, province, territory, emirate, or locality will be
chosen merely to make a jurisdiction classifiable. Where an official review
finds that operative rules require subnational specification absent from the
frozen scenario, the jurisdiction will be marked
`REQUIRES_SUBNATIONAL_SPECIFICATION` and held for human approval.
