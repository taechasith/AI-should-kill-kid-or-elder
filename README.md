# KA-IRO KRISIS v2

**KA-IRO KRISIS** is a newly coined research name inspired by the classical Greek concepts of *kairos*, the decisive moment for action, and *krisis*, judgment or decision at a turning point. Its intended meaning is “judgment at the critical moment”. It is a prospectively versioned research programme that tests whether semantic decision validity, interface-format reliability, and downstream physical safety diverge in safety-critical multimodal AI evaluation.

The earlier GeoSAVE Phase 4–9 pilot remains frozen, historically accurate, and reproducible. It is a feasibility foundation, not a dataset to be silently relabelled or modified by KA-IRO KRISIS v2. The current legal layer remains `NOT_ESTIMABLE`; map/road constraint compliance is not legal compliance.

The aspirational first-choice target is *Nature*; *Nature Machine Intelligence* is the fallback where the evidence is strong but specialist. See `docs/ka-iro-krisis/KA_IRO_KRISIS_V2_METHODOLOGY_GUIDE.md` for the governing protocol, `docs/ka-iro-krisis/KA_IRO_KRISIS_V2_NATURE_TARGET_AMENDMENT.md` for the prospective target change, and `docs/ka-iro-krisis/KA_IRO_KRISIS_V2_PREREGISTRATION.md` for the governing v2 specification.
